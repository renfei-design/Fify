# Code of conduct

Fify contributors are expected to make participation welcoming, respectful, and safe.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that strengthens the community include:

- Giving specific, constructive feedback about the work rather than the person.
- Respecting different design practices, technical backgrounds, identities, and levels of experience.
- Explaining decisions and tradeoffs so newcomers can participate.
- Protecting private information, credentials, and unpublished security reports.
- Accepting responsibility, apologizing, and repairing harm when mistakes happen.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, unwanted sexual attention, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers. Maintainers will acknowledge the report, protect confidentiality where possible, investigate proportionately, and communicate an outcome. Responses may include a private warning, removal of content, temporary participation restrictions, or a permanent ban.

Maintainers must recuse themselves when they are directly involved in a report. Until a dedicated conduct address is published, use GitHub's private **Report a vulnerability** flow described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and mark the report as a conduct concern.

This policy applies in project repositories, discussions, events, and other spaces where someone represents Fify.
